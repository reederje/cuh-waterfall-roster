How to Use This Interface

📋 Overview

This roster tracker displays all Emergency Department physicians grouped by area, showing their current shift status, patient assignments, and upcoming arrivals.

🎯 Recording Patient Assignments

  • Click on any physician to record that they've been assigned the next patient
  • The system will update their patient count and adjust the rotation order
  • Look for visual feedback when the update completes

✏️ Editing Patient Counts

  • Double-click on the patient count number to edit it manually
  • Type the correct number and press Enter to save
  • Press Escape to cancel without saving

📊 Understanding the Display

Currently present — Physicians actively on shift right now
  • Physicians assigned — Number of patients this physician is currently managing
  • Patients/hour — Calculated average based on shift duration and assignments
  • Shift time — Their scheduled shift hours (e.g., 08:00-16:00)

⏰ Arriving Soon Section

Shows physicians arriving within the next hour before their shift starts. This helps you prepare for upcoming coverage changes.

🌟 Supertrack Cards (Yellow Badge)

Areas marked with a ST badge use Supertrack logic:

  • Upward triangle (▲) — This physician is next in the rotation
  • Next assignments are selected based on patient rate and time spent on shift
  • This ensures fair distribution across the team

🔀 Selection Modes

Phase weighting (recommended):

  • Balances patient load and time on shift using adaptive weighting
  • Provides fairest distribution in most scenarios

Strict round robin:

  • Simple rotation through physicians in order
  • Each physician gets equal turns regardless of current load

🔄 Refresh and Updates

  • Click the ↻ Refresh button to manually fetch latest data
  • Data automatically refreshes every 15 minutes
  • The "Last updated" time in the header shows when data was last synced

⚠️ Tips & Troubleshooting

  • If you see an error banner, check your network connection and click Refresh
  • Patient counts are editable to correct data entry mistakes
  • The system is real-time—changes sync across all connected devices
  • If a physician appears faded/locked, an update is in progress—wait a moment
